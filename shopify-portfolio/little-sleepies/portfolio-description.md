# Little Sleepies — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Little Sleepies — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Baby & family sleepwear  
**Store Introduction:** A bamboo sleepwear brand selling baby zippies, two-piece pyjamas and matching family sets in limited-edition prints.

## My Contribution

Little Sleepies runs on print drops and family matching, which means the storefront has to sell the same pattern at three very different scales. The work focused on collection templates that keep a launch exciting without obscuring size and availability, family bundlers that make set assembly straightforward, and product cards that show the print clearly on baby, child and adult bodies. Sold-out states and restocks were treated as part of the experience rather than an error state.
