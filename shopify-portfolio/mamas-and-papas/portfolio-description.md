# Mamas & Papas — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Mamas & Papas — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Nursery & baby retail  
**Store Introduction:** A British nursery retailer selling pushchairs, car seats, furniture, clothing and baby essentials.

## My Contribution

Mamas & Papas sells a small number of expensive decisions, which calls for a different kind of storefront: less urgency, more guidance. The work focused on comparison that stays readable, compatibility between pushchairs, seats and bases, and a clear path from online research to a store appointment. Bulky-item delivery and assembly options were treated as part of the purchase rather than a postcode afterthought.
