# Modani — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Modani — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Modern furniture & home  
**Store Introduction:** A modern furniture retailer selling sofas, beds, dining and case goods online and through showrooms.

## My Contribution

The storefront work centred on the tension between a promotional calendar and a premium furniture catalogue. Page structure was organised so campaign messaging, room imagery and product detail each had a clear role, with consistent treatment of sale pricing and product crops across collection and product templates. Responsive behaviour received particular attention at the tablet widths where multi-column furniture grids tend to collapse, and the homepage was treated as a set of independently editable modules rather than one fixed composition.
