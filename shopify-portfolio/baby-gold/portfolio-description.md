# Baby Gold — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Baby Gold — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Personalised fine jewellery  
**Store Introduction:** A Los Angeles fine-jewellery brand specialising in 14k solid gold name necklaces, charms and personalised pieces.

## My Contribution

Baby Gold sells sentiment, which makes the personalisation interface the most important part of the page. The work focused on making that interface trustworthy: clear font and chain selection, validation before checkout, and pricing that reflects the configuration rather than appearing after the fact. Made-to-order and in-stock pieces were kept visually distinct, and lead times were stated where they affect a gift deadline, so the emotional decision is not undermined by logistics discovered too late.
