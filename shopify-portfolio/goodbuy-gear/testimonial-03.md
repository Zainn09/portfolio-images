# Testimonial Concept 03 — Overall Project & Shopping Experience

> **Suggested Testimonial Concept — Requires Genuine Client Review & Approval.**  
> The speaker below is a fictional placeholder, not a real client. Replace the profile with a real, consenting person before recording or publishing. Do not present this draft as an actual customer review.

**Suggested Speaker:** Community and resale operations lead — requires genuine client confirmation  
**Tone:** Friendly, pragmatic  
**Perspective:** Resale as a practical choice, not a compromise.  
**Target length:** 60–90 seconds

## Script

People come to resale for the price and stay for the reassurance.

The first question is always condition. The second is safety. If either feels vague, the saving doesn't matter.

What improved was how quickly those answers appeared. Condition was visible on the card, not buried three clicks in. Photos showed the actual item, including the scuffs. The comparison against the new price was stated plainly, without a fake countdown.

The seller side got simpler too. Parents have a garage full of gear they've stopped using, and the intake form now explains the process in a few steps instead of a wall of policy.

The marketplace feels less like a gamble now, and more like a sensible way to buy. That's the change I notice when I talk to sellers. They stop apologising for the scuff and start explaining why the stroller was worth buying in the first place.
