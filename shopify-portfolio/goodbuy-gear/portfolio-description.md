# GoodBuy Gear — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** GoodBuy Gear — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Baby gear resale  
**Store Introduction:** A resale marketplace for used, open-box and overstock baby gear, from strollers to nursery furniture.

## My Contribution

GoodBuy Gear's storefront has to make a single, used item feel as trustworthy as a new product with a warranty. The work focused on condition as a first-class attribute rather than a badge: consistent grading language, filters that reflect real availability, and product pages with enough photography and detail to substitute for handling the item. Single-unit inventory was treated as a design constraint, not an inconvenience, and the resale value proposition was kept factual rather than eco-sloganeering.
