# Albee Baby — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Albee Baby — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Baby gear retail  
**Store Introduction:** A family-owned New York baby retailer selling strollers, car seats, furniture and essentials online and in store.

## My Contribution

Albee Baby is an independent retailer competing with marketplaces, so the storefront has to combine breadth with judgement. The work focused on navigation that treats brand and category as equally valid entry points, product cards that stay consistent across a very large assortment, and promotional modules that do not overwhelm the advice-led tone. Registry and store information were kept within the shopping journey rather than relegated to utility pages.
