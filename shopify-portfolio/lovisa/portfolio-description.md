# Lovisa — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Lovisa — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Fashion jewellery  
**Store Introduction:** A trend-led fashion jewellery retailer selling earrings, body piercing jewellery and accessories online and through a large store network.

## My Contribution

Lovisa's catalogue moves faster than most jewellery stores, which makes consistency the hard part. The work focused on collection templates that can absorb weekly drops without losing hierarchy: new-in and best-seller rails with clear entry points, product cards that keep finish and pack count readable, and piercing-placement navigation that reflects how customers actually shop for body jewellery. The storefront keeps its loud, trend-led character while staying navigable on a phone.
