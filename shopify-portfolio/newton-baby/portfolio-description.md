# Newton Baby — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Newton Baby — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Baby sleep products  
**Store Introduction:** A baby sleep brand known for a breathable crib mattress and compatible bedding.

## My Contribution

Newton Baby sells a benefit you cannot see: airflow through a mattress core. The work focused on making that benefit concrete through cutaway imagery, plain-language material explanations and safety information placed before the purchase decision. Size variants and sheet compatibility were treated as part of the product, not an accessory afterthought, and bundles were presented as complete sleep setups rather than discounted line items.
