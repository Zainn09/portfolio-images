# Kyte Baby — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Kyte Baby — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Baby sleepwear  
**Store Introduction:** A bamboo sleepwear brand selling sleep bags, pyjamas and clothing for babies, children and adults.

## My Contribution

Kyte Baby's range is visually quiet and technically specific: the same simple shapes recur across sizes, colours and TOG ratings. The work focused on keeping that matrix clear, with warmth guidance attached to the right product, sold-out colourways handled cleanly, and collection pages that remain calm through frequent drops. The visual language stays close to the fabric, using texture and muted colour rather than decorative layout.
