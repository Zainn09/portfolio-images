# DeMellier London — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** DeMellier London — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Luxury handbags  
**Store Introduction:** A London handbag brand selling structured leather bags and small leather goods with a charitable giving model.

## My Contribution

DeMellier's storefront is built on restraint, which makes execution visible: spacing, image fidelity and typographic calm have nowhere to hide. The work focused on a product template where a single bag carries the page, dimensions and device fit sit within reach, and size families remain comparable without resetting leather or strap choices. Personalisation was treated as a variant with real rules and clear final-sale language rather than a field appended at checkout.
