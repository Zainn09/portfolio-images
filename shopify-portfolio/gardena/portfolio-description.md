# Gardena — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Gardena — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Garden equipment  
**Store Introduction:** A garden equipment brand covering watering, lawn care, robotic mowers and hand tools.

## My Contribution

This storefront carries an unusually wide technical range, from hand tools to app-connected robotic mowers, and the work focused on making that range legible. Product pages put family and purpose ahead of specification, then present the technical detail in a form that survives a narrow screen. Locale, units and availability were treated as one data problem rather than a currency switch, and comparison views were built to line up products whose attribute sets do not match.
