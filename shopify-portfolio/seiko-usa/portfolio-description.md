# Seiko USA — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Seiko USA — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Watches  
**Store Introduction:** The US storefront for Seiko watches, spanning Prospex, Presage, Astron, Seiko 5 and special editions.

## My Contribution

Seiko's storefront has to serve two audiences at once: someone buying a first automatic watch and someone comparing reference numbers. The work centred on structured product data, so collection filters and specification tables use real values rather than hand-written description copy. Collection templates give Prospex, Presage and Astron distinct characters within one system, while the dealer handoff stays explicit about where and how the watch can be bought.
