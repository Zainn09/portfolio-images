# Testimonial Concept 03 — Overall Project & Shopping Experience

> **Suggested Testimonial Concept — Requires Genuine Client Review & Approval.**  
> The speaker below is a fictional placeholder, not a real client. Replace the profile with a real, consenting person before recording or publishing. Do not present this draft as an actual customer review.

**Suggested Speaker:** Retail operations lead, authorised dealer network — requires genuine client confirmation  
**Tone:** Warm, observational  
**Perspective:** Making the online research translate into a better store visit.  
**Target length:** 60–90 seconds

## Script

People research a watch for weeks. They read reviews, compare movements, watch a video, then walk into a dealer and ask to see two models.

The website is part of that research, so its job is to answer the question they'll ask in the shop. How big is it on a wrist? What's the lug-to-lug? Does this bracelet taper?

What improved was the consistency of that information. You could compare a Prospex and a Presage without one page giving you case thickness and the other giving you a paragraph about heritage. The dealer locator also became a real step in the journey rather than a link in the footer.

We can't claim it increased showroom visits. I don't have that data. But the conversations feel different when someone arrives already knowing the reference number.

That's the test for me: does the site make the store visit better, not just the online order.

## Verification notes for this concept

The showroom observation is qualitative and requires approval. No visit or sales metric is claimed.
