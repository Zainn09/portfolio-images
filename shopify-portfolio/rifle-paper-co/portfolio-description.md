# Rifle Paper Co. — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Rifle Paper Co. — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Stationery, home & lifestyle  
**Store Introduction:** A lifestyle brand applying hand-painted artwork to stationery, wallpaper, textiles, accessories and gifts.

## My Contribution

Rifle Paper Co. is organised around artwork, so the storefront had to protect the illustrations rather than compete with them. The work centred on giving pattern a consistent role across categories: a shared taxonomy that lets a print move from wallpaper to stationery to apparel without losing its identity. Supporting details received equal attention, including stable crops and colour between thumbnails and product pages, clear separation of samples from full-size items, and personalisation fields that do not disturb the checkout.
