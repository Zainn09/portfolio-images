# Deryan — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Deryan — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Baby travel products  
**Store Introduction:** A Dutch brand selling pop-up travel cots, baby tents and compact camping beds for babies and toddlers.

## My Contribution

Deryan sells portability, so the storefront has to make a physical action obvious: the cot opening, the bag packing, the weight on a shoulder. The work focused on product pages that demonstrate the mechanism instead of describing it, a range structure that separates infant, toddler and tent formats, and a multilingual template that keeps specifications consistent across markets. Bundle contents and age suitability were kept close to the purchase decision.
