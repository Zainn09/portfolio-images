# Momcozy — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Momcozy — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Maternity & baby care  
**Store Introduction:** A maternity and baby brand selling wearable breast pumps, nursing essentials, postpartum care and baby monitoring products.

## My Contribution

Momcozy's customer is usually reading on a phone, one-handed, with very little patience for a marketing page. The work focused on short, scannable guidance: fit and sizing help close to the selector, pump comparisons built around the few differences that affect a decision, and bundles whose contents stay visible through the cart. The visual language stays calm and supportive, which matters for products that are intimate, clinical and emotional at the same time.
