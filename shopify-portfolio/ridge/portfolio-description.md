# Ridge — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Ridge — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Everyday carry & accessories  
**Store Introduction:** An everyday-carry brand selling slim wallets, rings, key cases, travel gear and personalisable metal accessories.

## My Contribution

Ridge sells a small number of shapes across a large matrix of materials, colours and kits, which puts unusual pressure on consistency. The work focused on keeping that matrix legible: product imagery and finish swatches that stay comparable, kits presented as real sets with visible contents, and an engraving flow that sets expectations before payment. The storefront keeps its hard-edged, material-led character while making the practical questions, what is included and what happens if it breaks, easy to answer.
