# Caden Lane — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Caden Lane — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Personalised baby gifts  
**Store Introduction:** A baby brand selling personalised swaddles, name blankets, newborn outfits and nursery keepsakes.

## My Contribution

Caden Lane sells a name before it sells a blanket. The work centred on making personalisation feel effortless and accurate: a preview that reflects font, colour and placement, validation that catches unsupported characters before an order reaches production, and a product page that keeps the print visible alongside the name. Gift bundles and lead times were treated as part of the emotional purchase, since a baby-shower gift that arrives late has already failed.
