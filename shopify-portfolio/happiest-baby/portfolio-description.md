# Happiest Baby — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Happiest Baby — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Baby sleep products  
**Store Introduction:** A baby sleep company selling the SNOO smart sleeper, swaddles, sleep aids and parent education.

## My Contribution

Happiest Baby sells a product that parents consider at their most vulnerable, so the storefront has to earn trust before it asks for a decision. The work focused on separating rental from purchase without burying either, keeping safety and return information near the choice, and shaping long-form education into short, scannable sections. The visual language stays calm and clinical without feeling cold, which suits a product that sits between medical advice and nursery furniture.
