# Lightbox Jewelry — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Lightbox Jewelry — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Lab-grown diamond jewellery  
**Store Introduction:** A lab-grown diamond jewellery brand selling studs, necklaces, rings and pendants with transparent per-carat pricing.

## My Contribution

Lightbox sells a product category that is usually wrapped in theatre, and the storefront's strength is its refusal to do that. The work centred on a calm product template where stone, metal and carat are legible at a glance, and where price sits beside the specification instead of behind an enquiry. Carat selection was treated as real inventory rather than a decorative control, with imagery and availability following the choice, and the visual language stayed close to the stones rather than borrowing traditional jewellery cues.
