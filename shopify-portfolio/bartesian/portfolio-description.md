# Bartesian — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Bartesian — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Home cocktail appliances  
**Store Introduction:** A home cocktail machine brand built around reusable spirit reservoirs and single-serve flavour capsules.

## My Contribution

Bartesian sells a system, not a single product, so the storefront has to explain the relationship between machine, capsule and spirit before it can sell any of them. The work focused on sequencing that explanation and on making the commercial options honest: bundle contents visible in the cart, subscription cadence chosen before checkout, and capsule information that separates flavour from strength. The result is a purchase path that answers the category's obvious questions in order rather than burying them in an FAQ.
