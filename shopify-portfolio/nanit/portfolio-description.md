# Nanit — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Nanit — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Smart baby monitors  
**Store Introduction:** A smart baby monitor brand combining an overhead camera, breathing wear and an app-based sleep insights subscription.

## My Contribution

Nanit sells a system in which the camera, the stand, the breathing wear and the app all have to make sense together. The work focused on explaining that system in layers: the core device first, the accessories that change how it is used second, and the optional subscription last. Bundles were treated as defined packages with visible contents, and subscription terms were stated at the point of choice rather than in fine print.
