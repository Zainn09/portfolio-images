# Body Candy — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Body Candy — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Body jewellery  
**Store Introduction:** A body jewellery retailer covering piercings, gauges, plugs and alternative accessories across a very large catalogue.

## My Contribution

Body Candy's catalogue is technical before it is fashionable: gauge, diameter, threading and material decide whether a product is usable. The work treated those attributes as the organising structure, so filters and product cards reflect how customers actually search, while the brand's loud, community-led tone survives in the imagery and typography. Sizing guidance sits close to the decision, variant changes preserve the selected size, and dense collection pages remain scannable on a phone.
