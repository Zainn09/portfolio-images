# Magnolia — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** Magnolia — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Home & lifestyle  
**Store Introduction:** A home and lifestyle brand spanning furniture, decor, paint, wallpaper, kitchen and garden.

## My Contribution

The work was framed around a catalogue that refuses to sit in one shape: furniture beside paint, wallpaper beside candles, editorial content beside product grids. Collection templates were approached as a shared system, with filters organised around decisions a customer actually makes, such as colour family, room and format. Paint and wallpaper received treatment appropriate to colour selection rather than generic product thumbnails, and the homepage kept its seasonal, magazine-like pacing without hiding the route into the catalogue.
