# Testimonial Concept 03 — Overall Project & Shopping Experience

> **Suggested Testimonial Concept — Requires Genuine Client Review & Approval.**  
> The speaker below is a fictional placeholder, not a real client. Replace the profile with a real, consenting person before recording or publishing. Do not present this draft as an actual customer review.

**Suggested Speaker:** Customer experience lead, home decor — requires genuine client confirmation  
**Tone:** Bright, conversational  
**Perspective:** How a customer wanders from inspiration to a coordinated room.  
**Target length:** 60–90 seconds

## Script

I shop our own site the way a customer does: on a phone, at night, with a half-formed idea.

The change I felt was in the browse. Before, if I liked a wallpaper, I had to go hunting for the bedding or the mugs that shared the pattern. Now the collection page gives me a way through by pattern and by room, so the site suggests the connection instead of making me guess.

The paint samples are a good example. You order a small sample, you wait, you hold it against the wall. That journey needs different information from buying a gallon. The page finally treats it that way.

This isn't a story about a metric. It's about the store feeling easier to wander through, and wandering is how people buy home decor.
