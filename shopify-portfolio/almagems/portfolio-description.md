# AlmaGems — Shopify Design & Development

**Project Title:** AlmaGems — Shopify Design & Development  
**Niche:** Personalised gifts  
**Store Introduction:** A personalised gift store spanning engraved leather bags, jewellery dishes, drinkware, ornaments and printed keepsakes.

## My Contribution

AlmaGems sells a feeling attached to an object, and the catalogue spans engraving, printing and embroidery, so the storefront has to make personalisation feel consistent across very different products. The work focused on a shared personalisation pattern with production-appropriate previews, occasion-led navigation that meets gift buyers where they start, and product photography that keeps the message legible. Discount and multi-buy mechanics were kept clear at the cart rather than layered on as surprises.
