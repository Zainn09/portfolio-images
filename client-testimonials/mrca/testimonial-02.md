# Testimonial 2 — Suggested corporate marketing lead or communications manager

**Perspective:** Specific project improvement. This is a first-person draft for a real client to correct, personalise and approve.

**Approximate duration:** 60–75 seconds  
**Tone:** precise, conversational  
**Location / framing:** standing or interview-style, off-camera interviewer.  
**Delivery:** Natural pace; pause after the first sentence and keep technical terms in the speaker's own words.  
**B-roll:** screen recording, mobile test, product/category page, issue reproduction.  
**Seated / standing / walking:** standing or interview-style.

## Script

> One thing that stood out to me was how quickly the review moved from general impressions to a real customer journey on Manufacturing Revitalization Corporation of America.
>
> Abdul Rehman's review helped us look at the site as a real person would use it. For Manufacturing Revitalization Corporation of America, that meant paying attention to enterprise narrative and portfolio architecture. The useful thing was the way he separated what he could actually observe from what still needed checking. He didn't present a guess as a result, and he didn't promise a sales lift that we hadn't measured.
>
> He walked through the relevant pages, recorded the points that could cause confusion, and explained why each one mattered. **We could finally see where the experience needed attention.** That made the conversation much easier for our team because we had specific examples rather than a long list of vague recommendations.
>
> The next step is to confirm which fixes were completed and what changed after re-testing. But even before that, the work gave us a clearer view of making a complex portfolio story easier to follow. I would describe it as a practical review that helped us decide what to investigate and improve next.

**Key sentence to emphasise:** “We could finally see where the experience needed attention.”
