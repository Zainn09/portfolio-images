# Testimonial 1 — Suggested founder or small-business e-commerce lead

**Perspective:** Personal experience. This is a first-person draft for a real client to correct, personalise and approve.

**Approximate duration:** 60–75 seconds  
**Tone:** calm, reflective  
**Location / framing:** seated, direct-to-camera.  
**Delivery:** Natural pace; pause after the first sentence and keep technical terms in the speaker's own words.  
**B-roll:** desk/work materials, annotated page, call notes, before/after issue list.  
**Seated / standing / walking:** seated.

## Script

> Before this review, we knew the making a warm local brand easier to shop mattered, but we didn't have a very clear way to talk about the gaps.
>
> Abdul Rehman's review helped us look at the site as a real person would use it. For Cuddles Dog Bakery, that meant paying attention to local-pickup clarity. The useful thing was the way he separated what he could actually observe from what still needed checking. He didn't present a guess as a result, and he didn't promise a sales lift that we hadn't measured.
>
> He walked through the relevant pages, recorded the points that could cause confusion, and explained why each one mattered. **The useful part was having the problems explained clearly, not just pointed out.** That made the conversation much easier for our team because we had specific examples rather than a long list of vague recommendations.
>
> The next step is to confirm which fixes were completed and what changed after re-testing. But even before that, the work gave us a clearer view of making a warm local brand easier to shop. I would describe it as a practical review that helped us decide what to investigate and improve next.

**Key sentence to emphasise:** “The useful part was having the problems explained clearly, not just pointed out.”
