# Claims to Verify — BalletPro

No testimonial in this folder should be published as a client statement until a real speaker confirms the wording.

## Confirm before recording
- The identity, name, title and company of the speaker.
- Whether Abdul Rehman worked on this website, and the exact scope: audit, QA, UX, technical review, issue reporting, prioritisation or re-testing.
- Which observations were actually found during the project, on which pages/devices, and which fixes were made.
- Any claim about improved speed, conversion, revenue, sales, traffic, rankings, accessibility compliance or reduced support requests.
- Whether the site facts used in the draft are still current: Shopify storefront with collection-led navigation, product reviews, stockist search, trade enquiry and a prominent set of customer quotes. The page names Amanda and team in one quote but does not establish a project relationship with Abdul.
- Any numerical or customer proof displayed on the public site. Treat it as a company-published claim, not as a result of Abdul's work.

## Safe publishing note
The scripts are **drafts, not fabricated testimonials**. Replace generic passages with the client's own words and remove anything they cannot personally verify.
