# Testimonial 3 — Suggested founder or e-commerce manager

**Perspective:** Team perspective. This is a first-person draft for a real client to correct, personalise and approve.

**Approximate duration:** 60–75 seconds  
**Tone:** practical, lightly upbeat  
**Location / framing:** walking through workspace with occasional cutaways.  
**Delivery:** Natural pace; pause after the first sentence and keep technical terms in the speaker's own words.  
**B-roll:** ticket or checklist, team discussion, responsive layouts, retest notes.  
**Seated / standing / walking:** walking through workspace with occasional cutaways.

## Script

> For our team, the value wasn't a big headline number. It was having someone look at the site with fresh eyes and give us something we could work from.
>
> Abdul Rehman's review helped us look at the site as a real person would use it. For Faraway Finds, that meant paying attention to internationalisation and first-load clarity. The useful thing was the way he separated what he could actually observe from what still needed checking. He didn't present a guess as a result, and he didn't promise a sales lift that we hadn't measured.
>
> He walked through the relevant pages, recorded the points that could cause confusion, and explained why each one mattered. **It gave the team a shared list of things to resolve.** That made the conversation much easier for our team because we had specific examples rather than a long list of vague recommendations.
>
> The next step is to confirm which fixes were completed and what changed after re-testing. But even before that, the work gave us a clearer view of finding and prioritising storefront issues where evidence is limited. I would describe it as a practical review that helped us decide what to investigate and improve next.

**Key sentence to emphasise:** “It gave the team a shared list of things to resolve.”
