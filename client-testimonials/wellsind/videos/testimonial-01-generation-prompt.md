# Video 01 generation prompt — Wells Industries

Create a fictional presenter / demo speaker testimonial. Do not imply this person is a real client. The client must approve the script, identity treatment and project claims before publication. Do not imitate a real person or use a real client's likeness or voice.

**Presenter:** marketing manager, clearly labelled “Fictional presenter — demo only”. Use a unique synthetic voice, distinct from every other project and from Video 02; natural accent, breathing, blinking, subtle head movement, small posture shifts and restrained hand gestures. Conversational, not an advert.
**Story arc:** Situation → problem → discovery → action → reaction.
**Environment:** design studio. Do not reuse the other video's set, clothing, framing or lighting.
**Camera:** 4K-style natural skin texture, shallow but believable depth of field, no glossy commercial look. Medium close-up, 16:9, daylight.
**B-roll:** laptop showing permitted public site pages; annotated QA notes; mobile layout review. Use only actual public website screens or client-supplied material. No invented analytics, dashboards, outcomes or numbers.
**Audio:** clean dialogue, subtle room tone, no overpowering music, accurate lip sync.
**Subtitles:** accurate verbatim captions, high contrast, bottom safe area, no kinetic typography.
**Truth constraint:** only use the attached finalized script. Retain language that says claims require confirmation. Never state that Abdul increased revenue, sales, traffic, conversion, speed or rankings unless the client adds dated evidence.
**Deliverable:** H.264 MP4, 1920x1080, 24/25/30 fps, AAC audio, 45–90 seconds.
**Vertical variant:** reframe the subject intelligently for 1080x1920, 9:16; preserve hands and relevant B-roll where possible.
