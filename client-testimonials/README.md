# Client Testimonials and Review

A separate, Git-ready set of testimonial research and video-script drafts for ten client websites. The material is designed to help a real client review, correct, personalise and approve a recording. It is not a collection of invented endorsements.

## Methodology

Public homepages were reviewed individually on 2026-10-03. Company descriptions and visible product/service facts are recorded in each `research.md`. Reasonable UX observations are kept separate from verified facts. No sales, revenue, conversion, speed, ranking or traffic improvement is claimed without supplied evidence.

## Sprint structure

- Sprint 1: Limitless Brain Lab, Enerex, BalletPro
- Sprint 2: Cuddles Dog Bakery, MYZO Chocolate, Bidet Toilets Store
- Sprint 3: Faraway Finds, MRCA
- Sprint 4: Wells Industries, Wisconic

## Status

All 30 scripts are **draft — client confirmation required**. Speaker profiles are suggestions only. No name, title or company has been assigned to an unverified speaker.

## Folder contents

Each project folder contains `research.md`, three distinct `testimonial-0N.md` scripts and `claims-to-verify.md`. Use [`TESTIMONIAL-INDEX.md`](TESTIMONIAL-INDEX.md) to navigate all projects.

## Verification requirements

1. Confirm that Abdul Rehman worked on the project and define his actual contribution.
2. Ask the real client to rewrite any sentence that does not sound like them.
3. Confirm every issue, fix and project detail against tickets, recordings or acceptance notes.
4. Add only measured KPI results with a dated source and permission to publish.
5. Confirm speaker name, title, company, release consent and usage rights.
6. Remove placeholders and any claim the speaker cannot personally stand behind.

## Publishing checklist

- [ ] Client approved the final words and approved the project description.
- [ ] Speaker identity and release are documented.
- [ ] Claims have evidence or have been softened to observations.
- [ ] Medical, product, shipping, guarantee and performance claims are checked with the relevant owner.
- [ ] Captions, transcript, audio, contrast, readable cutaways and B-roll permissions are complete.
- [ ] Final video has a project label and no unsupported before/after implication.
